# OncoAI — Clinical AI Infrastructure & Multi-Agentic Architecture

Ahluwalia Strategic Ventures | Enterprise Healthcare AI

---

## 🌐 Vision & Objective
OncoAI provides secure, HIPAA-compliant, private multi-agentic AI infrastructure for oncology clinical decision support, diagnostic synthesis, and therapeutic research workflows.

---

## ⚙️ Core Technical Pillars

### 1. Private Cloud & Data Sovereignty
* **Deployment Model:** NVIDIA NIM (Inference Microservices) on private cloud / on-premises GPU clusters.
* **Compliance:** Fully isolated data pipeline ensuring EHR data and patient identifiers remain within protected boundaries.

### 2. Multi-Agentic Reasoning (OpenClaw Engine)
* **Orchestration:** Multi-agent inference pipeline capable of parallelizing clinical trial lookup, genomic variant interpretation, and treatment guidelines.
* **API Gateway:** Managed routing via secure `ai-gateway-proxy`.

### 3. Safety & Guardrails (NeMo Framework)
* **Execution Boundary:** NeMo Guardrails enforce strict policy checks on output generation.
* **Deterministic Verification:** Eliminates off-label hallucinations and demands verified clinical citations.
